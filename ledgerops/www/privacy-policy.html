LedgerOps · Early access

Privacy notice for demo requests

When you submit the LedgerOps demo request form, we collect the name, business email, company, country, phone number (if supplied), approximate team/client counts and the workflow message you choose to provide.

Why we use this information

We use these details to respond to your demo request, assess product fit and communicate about LedgerOps early access. We do not ask for bank statements, customer financial records, tax identifiers or identity documents in the demo form.

Access and retention

Demo-request information is stored in the LedgerOps customer relationship management system and is accessible to authorized product and sales personnel. A production retention period, legal entity details, privacy contact and applicable regional disclosures must be completed by the operator before public commercial launch.

Your choices

You may ask the LedgerOps operator to correct or delete your demo-request information, subject to applicable legal requirements. The operator must publish a monitored privacy contact before accepting public requests at scale.

Important: This is an early-access notice template, not a legal determination or a complete privacy policy. Replace it with a reviewed notice identifying the operating legal entity, contact details, retention schedule, processors, international transfers and applicable rights before launching publicly.
